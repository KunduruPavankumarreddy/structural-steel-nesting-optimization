# Client B — Management Note
## A measured first step on material waste

You have identified nesting as the area where you would most like to start. Today, nesting is largely planned by hand, but StruMIS already has an automatic option. Before recommending a new system, I suggest we compare the two methods using the same jobs and agree on how material use should be measured.

### What we propose

First, spend **1–2 weeks** reviewing a small sample of drawing, nesting, stock, scrap and offcut records with your Cost Control, nesting, engineering and IT teams. This will show which figures can be trusted and establish the baseline.

If the data supports it, we would then run a **6–8 week pilot**. It will compare manual nesting, StruMIS automatic nesting and, only if needed, a constrained optimisation approach. The nesting engineer will review each recommendation; nothing will be sent directly to CNC machines by the pilot. We will also test offcut matching where records are reliable.

### What we need from you

Access to agreed historical jobs and their actual material outcomes, one point of contact for StruMIS/PLM exports, and working sessions with the nesting lead and engineering team. Cost Control will need to confirm material costs and how scrap credits should be treated. Cross-project material transfers will remain out of scope until your team approves the accounting rules.

### Timing and indicative cost

| Stage | Timing | Fee |
|---|---|---:|
| Discovery and data audit | 1–2 weeks | ₹3 lakh |
| Nesting pilot | 6–8 weeks | ₹12 lakh |
| Production integration, only after approval | 8–12 weeks | ₹20 lakh |
| Total through first production release | Subject to the pilot gate | **₹35 lakh** |

Fees exclude GST, third-party licence upgrades, hardware and major changes outside the agreed scope. Initial hosting and monitoring are estimated at ₹25,000–₹65,000 per month; ongoing support would be priced separately.

We will not promise a savings figure before reviewing your data. The pilot will report material utilisation, scrap and offcut reuse, plan feasibility, planning time and the net value calculated using your costs. We will recommend production rollout only if the improvement is repeatable, safe for the shop floor and financially worthwhile.

**Decision requested:** approve the discovery phase. At its end, you will have a data-backed recommendation on whether the pilot is worth funding.
